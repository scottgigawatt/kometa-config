# Code of conduct

Everyone should be able to participate without harassment. Technical disagreements belong in the review, not in attacks on the person.

## Our commitment

Welcome people regardless of experience, age, disability, ethnicity, nationality, appearance, religion, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, or other personal characteristics.

Be patient, give constructive feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and focus on evidence. Respect privacy and the time of contributors and maintainers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, slurs, threats, stalking, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disruption, and retaliation against good-faith reporters are not welcome. Do not publish another person's private information or expose credentials, private service addresses, or sensitive logs.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository contributions, issues, pull requests, and project community spaces, including when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Report a concern

Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive public content. For private concerns, request a private conversation with the repository owner or a HADES moderator without posting sensitive details. Do not put private conduct reports into public issues.

The repository owner is responsible for enforcement. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with information shared only as needed to investigate or respond. Platform rules and legal obligations still apply.

Security vulnerabilities use the separate [security reporting route](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and context, maintainers may request a correction, remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or block participation permanently. Immediate protective action may be taken when delay risks harm.

## Attribution

This project-specific policy draws on the principles and structure of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It is maintained and enforced by this project's owner.
